# Grit

Get better than you were yesterday. A running tracker built around personal bests (1 mi, 5K, 10K, longest run, best pace), streaks and a ghost runner that races your best effort on each route. Also: shoe mileage, race countdown, saved routes, weigh-ins with 5-lb milestones, water vs Diet Coke, and a weekly recap. Open it on your phone and use **Add to Home Screen**.

- Everything you log stays in your browser on your device (localStorage). Nothing is uploaded.
- Online extra: run-weather tips from Open-Meteo.
- Font: Inter (SIL Open Font License), embedded in `index.html`.
