# Fitness Arcade 🕹️👟

An '80s-themed running, calorie, weight, and hydration tracker. Open it on your phone and use **Add to Home Screen**.

- Everything you log stays in your browser on your device (localStorage). Nothing is uploaded.
- Online extras: weather tips from Open-Meteo, plus food lookups from USDA FoodData Central and Open Food Facts.
- Fonts: Press Start 2P and Monoton (SIL Open Font License), embedded in `index.html`.
